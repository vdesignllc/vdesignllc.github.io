519 on Broadway is a new building of boutique luxury condos on the Somerville and Medford line. I designed its logo, the logos for the realtor and the builder, and the pieces that sold the units.

Project

519 on Broadway, Somerville, Massachusetts

Clients

Partner

Delivered

Three logos, site banners, brochure, folder, open house program, print ads, web banners, social posts, website

Web banner. The rooftop terrace also leads the website and the Playbill cover.

Three logos, one job

Three names had to work together on every piece. The building, 519 on Broadway. The realtor selling the units, Mainstreet Residential Realty. The builder, Legacy Building & Development.

I designed all three marks during the project, as each one was needed. All three use a clean sans, so they sit together on a brochure back or the foot of an ad.

Mainstreet Residential Realty, 519 on Broadway and Legacy Building & Development.

Site banners

The first banner went up at the start of construction. The name and the web address, big enough to read from the street.

As the 3D renderings came together and units were reserved presale, two new banners showed the building, the prices and the amenities. One hung on the Broadway side, the other on Medford Street.

Construction site banner.
Presale banners. The vertical one hung from the top of the building on Broadway, the horizontal one on the Medford Street side.

The presale open house

The presale open house program was a Playbill. The cover sells the rooftop. Inside, the building is introduced like a production, with 55 residential units, 4 commercial spaces and 70+ parking spots.

Cast & Crew lists every unit. Who’s Who introduces the people behind the building. End Notes covers the rest.

Presale open house program. Cover, opening spread, Cast & Crew, and Who’s Who.

The brochure

The brochure cover puts the building against a bright sky. The spread inside sets it in front of the Boston skyline and makes four points with four icons. World-class amenities, a quick commute, a prime location and a premier neighborhood.

Brochure cover, spread and back.
Presentation folder with inserts on the kitchens, appliances and acoustic insulation.

Print ads

The open house ads ran in three sizes, full page, half page and quarter page, from one layout. The interiors change and the logos stay in the same place.

Open house ads, full page, half page and quarter page.

Launch ads and banners

The launch ads announced the debut. The web banners carried one line, Boutique Luxury Condos, and one location, on the Somerville/Medford line.

Launch ads, Boutique Condos Debut on Broadway.

The website

The site opened on the rooftop terrace with a button to schedule a tour. Inside pages covered the kitchens, the baths and local culture.

Home page and interior pages.

Instagram posts, before opening and for the winter move-in special.
