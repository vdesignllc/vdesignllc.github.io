Virginia Cox
Founder & Creative Director

Founded in 1996, V Design represents Virginia’s ideal vision of what a design and communications company should be — a blend of top talent, a scale that allows clients to benefit from a close and collaborative relationship with all team members, and an environment that leads to the creation of meaningful and compelling solutions.

For nearly 30 years, Virginia has translated complex business needs and goals into arresting, compelling, and results-driven solutions. Behind every project is the essence of her design thinking.

Every client stands as a reference. Every one.

More than 300 organizations since 1996, and over 90% of new work arrives by referral — which is a way of saying the studio has never really had to sell itself.

The range is the point. A Fortune 1000 diagnostics company. A national health nonprofit. A neighborhood school. What connects them is the problem underneath: an organization with something genuinely valuable to say and no reliable way to make an audience understand it.

Research graphics

Stevens Institute of Technology · 2018

I designed the cover and figures for a research roadmap on sensemaking. Stevens led it with USC, the University of Maryland and Virginia Tech. Sensemaking is how analysts pull many kinds of information together into a few working hypotheses. My figures showed that process. One funnels hundreds of sources down to a single analyst. One stacks the source types in layers. One maps the research over the next ten years.

The report was prepared for a federal client, so the work is not shown here.

Volunteer

Massachusetts
Foundation for Belmont Education Board member

I built the brand standard and a set of templates the board could reuse each year, customize, or run straight with new information dropped in. I also tracked down high resolution artwork for every donor and set up a shared asset library so the executive board could find sponsor logos and files for web, social, email and press without having to ask anyone. It has since moved onto their own platform and is still in use.

Friends of Belmont Soccer President 2020 to 2023. Chair of Fundraising 2015 to 2019.

I designed the mark and rebuilt a website that was telling the soccer community nothing and recruiting nobody, which is what a volunteer run organization lives on. Fundraising grew more than 200 percent in two years. We got there by going past the current roster to former players, their parents, and families new to the program, bringing coaches into the meetings, and giving teams an incentive to take part. I stepped down in 2023 and stayed on through the handover until new parent volunteers were ready to take it.

Belmont Middle and High School Building Project Advisory committee

District enrollment had climbed by 631 students in six years with about a thousand more projected. The answer was one building for grades 7 through 12 rather than a high school on its own. I sat on the advisory committee from the conceptual work through construction, in a room that mixed educators, architects, town officials and residents. It opened in 2023 for 2,215 students at a cost of roughly $295 million, about $80 million of it from the state.

Project Literacy Watertown Free Public Library

I tutor adults in English. Reading, writing, speaking, pronunciation and comprehension. The service is free to anyone in the community.
